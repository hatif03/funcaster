# Startup plan: helping indie developers find players and earn, on Arc and ENS

6 November 2026 at the owner's request: *research how AAA games make money with microtransactions and loot boxes, adapt it so that indie developers can use it without compromising their art, help them with distribution and players, and plan the next 3 to 4 months, especially on Arc and ENS.* It builds on [business-plan.md](../../../business-plan.md) and the web3 [build guide](../web3/build-guide/00-README.md) and does not replace them.

**The recommendation in four sentences.** Distribution is what the median indie lacks, so build the audience engine first. Give creators a fair store (tips, cosmetics, passes, priced in rupees, no virtual currency, invisible to the game's rules) second. Treat loot boxes as a staged, opt-in, capped, cosmetic-only and verifiably fair feature that ships only with a counsel opinion in hand, starting with free earned drops. Use Arc and ENS as plumbing the player never sees: instant payouts and splits, an auditable odds log, and portable studio names.

Figures are tagged [P] primary, [S] secondary, [U] assumption. Nothing here is legal or tax advice.
