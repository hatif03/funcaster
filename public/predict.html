How it works

Everyone’s predictions go into one pool. Winners keep their stake and share the losing side’s coins. Tied winners share the profit. When nobody picks the other side, coins come back.

Predictions close before play. A separate process replays the game; results need matching reports. These two checks are operated by Funcaster, not independent organizations. Seated players cannot predict their own match.

Your phone holds its own identity. Keep this browser’s data to collect your coins. Local play coins are a demonstration ledger; they are not USDC and cannot be withdrawn.
